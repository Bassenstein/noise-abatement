LGen linear reciprocating motion is a tonal concern — Mainspring units require acoustic enclosure with 10 dB(A) attenuation. Compressor blade-pass frequency for GT must be below audible tonal threshold at fence line.
§551.02 Measurement Protocol: Sound level measurements shall be taken with a Type 1 or Type 2 sound level meter meeting ANSI S1.4 standards, A-weighted slow response. Measurements taken at or beyond the property line, 4–5 ft above grade, minimum 3 ft from reflective surfaces. Average of three 1-minute Leq readings constitutes a valid sample. Meteorological conditions: wind <5 m/s, no precipitation, >10 dB background ambient confirmation required.

Morabito Class 4 Acoustic Package: ~$13.8M covering (1) GT exhaust silencer stack, (2) HRSG duct silencers, (3) cooling tower fill and fan acoustic enclosures, (4) LGen building enclosures, (5) HV transformer enclosure on E. 55th side, (6) perimeter concrete noise barrier (north & west), (7) continuous fence-line monitoring stations (×6). Targeting full §551 compliance with ≥5 dB headroom on all residential boundaries.
